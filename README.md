# Deedee.run

**Give Deedee a file. Get clean data in seconds.**

Deedee is a private AI workspace for turning business documents into structured, verifiable data.

Upload a PDF, spreadsheet, image, or business file. Deedee extracts fields, entities, relationships, and summaries while keeping every result connected to its original source, so you can verify the answer instead of blindly trusting it.

[Visit Deedee.run](https://deedee.run) · [Explore the documentation](https://deedee.run/docs)

---

## What we build

* **Document intake**
  Process PDFs, spreadsheets, Office documents, images, CSV, JSON, and other business file formats.

* **Structured extraction**
  Extract vendors, invoice details, amounts, dates, line items, clauses, entities, and other useful fields.

* **Evidence-linked results**
  Keep extracted data and AI answers connected to the source page or document section they came from.

* **Workspace chat**
  Ask questions across uploaded files, extracted content, structured data, and connected entities.

* **Business context**
  Organize relationships between people, businesses, invoices, accounts, and cash movements in one workspace.

* **Agent integrations**
  Connect Deedee workflows with AI agents, development tools, and Model Context Protocol clients.
